Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ownership, home financing, possession, and upkeep.

Phase 1 residences of Grah Heights are in handover phase for ready-to-move buyers. Phase 2 towers have possession scheduled for December 2025. Grah Enclave independent row houses are already structurally ready for interior fit-outs and immediate registry with immediate handover on full payment.
Both Grah Heights and Grah Enclave have institutional project approvals from India's leading financial institutions including State Bank of India (SBI), Punjab National Bank (PNB), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Bank of Baroda. Eligible buyers can avail up to 90% financing at prevailing competitive home loan interest rates with customized EMI structures.
Every 2 & 3 BHK apartment in Grah Heights is allotted designated covered basement or stilt car parking with numbered bays. Grah Enclave independent row houses come with private in-compound covered porches capable of parking 1-2 large SUVs. Dedicated outdoor visitor parking bays with 24x7 security monitoring are situated right by the main gate complex.
Maintenance charges are pegged nominally to cover 24x7 security personnel, high-speed lift upkeep, common area generator diesel backup, landscaping, and waste management. During the initial handover period, Grah Developers' in-house property care team manages all operations, following which responsibility seamlessly transitions to the officially formed Resident Welfare Association (RWA).
