Our turnkey home construction rates in Lucknow start from ₹1,650 per sq. ft. for standard quality and ₹1,950 – ₹2,200 per sq. ft. for luxury specifications. This includes architectural drawings, foundation and RCC slab casting, brickwork, premium plumbing (Jaquar), Havells electrical wiring, double-charge vitrified tiles, and weather-proof exterior painting.
Payments are completely milestone-based, protecting your finances: 10% on booking & architectural design, 15% on foundation completion, 20% on ground floor slab casting, 15% on first floor slab, 15% on brickwork & plastering, 15% on flooring & plumbing, and the final 10% on final possession handover with structural certificate.
Every property and development under Shilanyaas undergoes rigorous legal scrutiny with a 30-year title verification, non-encumbrance certificate, and municipal clearances. Our dedicated legal desk assists you through the Sub-Registrar office registry and ensures subsequent municipal mutation (dakhil kharij) documentation without procedural delays.
We provide a written 10-Year RCC Structural Warranty covering columns, beams, roof slabs, and foundation integrity. Furthermore, all branded installations come with original manufacturer warranties — 10 years for Jaquar/Cera bath fittings, 10 years for concealed anti-leak CPVC pipes, and 5-7 years on exterior weather-coat paint finishes.
