TRANSPARENCY & LEGALITIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about registry, legal approvals, revenue mutation, and bank home loans before booking your plot.

All plots under Metro Estate Developers are 100% Freehold Bhumidhari lands with clear, undisputed 30-year chain of title. The sale deed (Registry) is executed directly in your name at the official Sub-Registrar Office in Mohanlalganj, Lucknow. You receive the authentic stamped government registered deed along with on-spot possession and physical demarcation pillars.
Our plotted townships strictly follow the Zila Panchayat planning norms and Lucknow Master Plan 2031 residential land-use zoning. The layout adheres to all mandatory parameters including generous 40-ft and 30-ft road setbacks, open parks, transformer areas, and storm drainage easements, safeguarding your investment against unauthorized development risks.
Yes, absolutely! Since our projects possess complete title verification and khatauni records, leading nationalized and private banks—including State Bank of India (SBI), Punjab National Bank (PNB), Bank of Baroda, and HDFC—provide plot purchase loans and composite plot+construction loans up to 75%–80% of property valuation with minimal processing times.
Immediately upon registry, our in-house legal liaison team initiates the formal Dakhil Kharij (Revenue Mutation) application in the government UP Bhulekh revenue portal (Tehsil Mohanlalganj). The mutation order updating your name in the government Khatauni is systematically accomplished within 35 to 45 business days, ensuring complete ownership transfer.
Possession is immediate upon signing the registry agreement. Our township surveyor installs reinforced RCC corner pillars with your plot number on the very same day. Since wide roads, underground electricity, and water supply are already in place, buyers can immediately construct their boundary wall, borewell, or commence residential construction without delay.
